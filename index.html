Remote Desktop Operations

This is where Supremo comes in. You'll learn to set up, configure, and manage remote support sessions end-to-end — from unattended access configuration to session recording and compliance logging. We chose Supremo because it's lightweight enough to deploy in under five minutes but has the depth for enterprise-scale operations. The lab exercises use real multi-site scenarios: a main office, two branch offices, and a handful of remote workers. How Supremo works — and why we teach it

We picked Supremo as our remote desktop platform for the labs because it hits the sweet spot between simplicity and real-world capability. Here's what our students actually work with:

1

Connect in Seconds

Supremo generates a unique ID and password for each machine. Enter the remote machine's ID, authenticate, and you're connected. No complex network configuration, no VPN tunnels to set up first — though our labs do teach you when those matter.

2

Unattended Access & Address Books

For managed environments, Supremo supports unattended access — set it once, connect anytime without someone on the other end clicking "allow." The address book organizes machines by group, which matters when you're managing 50+ endpoints across sites.

3

Session Control & Audit

File transfers mid-session, multi-monitor support, chat, and — the part compliance officers care about — full session recording and connection logs. Our lab exercises walk through configuring these for a regulated environment where audit trails aren't optional.

Why Supremo specifically?

Look, there are plenty of remote desktop tools out there. We went with Supremo for three practical reasons: it runs without requiring a dedicated server infrastructure, the licensing model works for training environments, and it covers the feature set that most mid-market IT teams actually use — without the complexity overhead of enterprise-tier platforms that take weeks to deploy properly.

That said, our remote desktop track teaches concepts that transfer to any tool. The muscle memory around session security, unattended access policies, and remote troubleshooting workflows — that part is tool-agnostic. Supremo is just the vehicle we use to teach it.

Questions we hear most

What experience level do I need?

At least two years in IT operations. You should know your way around a command line. We're not a helpdesk-to-sysadmin bridge — our labs assume baseline server management skills. Career changers with some hands-on experience have done well, but talk to us first so we can be honest about fit.

How does the Supremo training work?

It's part of the Remote Desktop Operations track. You connect to lab machines through Supremo during exercises — setting up unattended access, managing address books, running supervised remote support sessions, configuring session recording. All browser-accessible. No local setup required on your end.

What does it cost?

It depends on the track, cohort size, and how many people you're enrolling. Request a quote — we'll give you specific numbers, not a generic brochure. Payment plans are available, and about a third of our students get their employer to cover it.

Is everything remote?

Yes. Labs, sessions, office hours — all remote since 2020. You access lab environments through your browser. We used to run in-person sessions in Portland, and honestly the remote format ended up working better for working professionals.

Will this prepare me for vendor certifications?

We don't teach to any specific exam. But the skills overlap is real — roughly 60% of graduates pursue a certification afterward and tell us the practical experience made prep significantly easier.

What if I fall behind?

Roll into the next cohort. No extra charge. It happens — life gets complicated. We've had people do this twice. No judgment.
